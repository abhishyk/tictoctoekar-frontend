ℹ️ Help & Features

🤝 Invite Friend / Find Player

Search a player's username, or share your invite link so a friend can join directly. Both players stake 10 coins to start — the winner gets their own 10 back (no extra gain), the loser's 10 coins are forfeited. A genuine draw refunds both players, nobody loses anything.

⚡ Auto Match

Same 10-coin stake and rules as Invite Friend — instantly pairs you with whoever else is looking for a match right now, no searching needed.

🤖 Beat the Bot, Earn Coins

Free practice against the AI — no entry fee, no risk. Win and you earn +3 coins (up to 300 coins/day). A draw or a loss costs nothing. Bot games never count towards your Wins or the Leaderboard — they're for earning coins only, not for climbing the rankings.

💰 Wallet & running out of coins

Your balance, match history and wins live in Wallet. If you drop to 0 coins you can't join a paid match (Invite Friend / Auto Match need 10) — but Beat the Bot, Earn Coins is always free and can top you back up, or buy coins instantly with Telegram Stars from Wallet → Buy Coins.

🎡 Spin & Win Reward

Unlocks once your wallet hits 1,00,000 coins. Each spin costs a flat 99,000 coins (so at exactly 1,00,000 you're left with 1,000 after spinning). Prizes: 3 Months Telegram Premium, 15/25/50/100 Telegram Stars, an Admin Choice surprise, or Better Luck Next Time. Real prizes (not coins) aren't auto-delivered — you'll get a message here once the team credits it.

🏆 Leaderboard

Ranked purely by PvP wins (Invite Friend / Auto Match). Bot-mode games are excluded on purpose, so the leaderboard always reflects real matches against real players.
